# Android demo catalog expansion

Four stable seller IDs each seed exactly 18 menu items: `main-canteen`, `north-counter`, `coffee-corner`, `campus-pantry`. This is **72 distinct Android sample items**: the original 12 IDs retain their seller associations, and 60 new IDs have been added. Prices, menu availability, hours and outlet identities are illustrative, not verified merchant listings. `lemon-rice` deliberately remains unavailable.

The taxonomy separates food category from preparation/packaging. Stable `drinks` displays as **Beverages** and stable `packs` as **Packaged Snacks**. Pantry fruit juice and water both use `drinks` + `PACKAGED`; fresh filter coffee uses `drinks` + `PREPARED`. `idli` is Breakfast, `corner-biscuits` is Cookies & Biscuits, and North lunch is Combos. The existing quote/promotion IDs remain stable; the North lunch sample promotion now points to Combos. Added category IDs are `breakfast`, `meals`, `chaat`, `rolls`, `sandwiches`, `combos`, `puffs`, `breads`, `cakes`, and `cookies`.

Customer category chips are derived from visible items of the selected seller, with invalid selections cleared after edits. Search supports seller, category, query, and independent `FoodKind` conjunctions. Seller menu management shows bundled per-item imagery, respects local photo overrides, and resets to the 72-item seed. Main Canteen prepared items retain individual-demo eligibility; all packaged items and all secondary-seller items remain bulk-only.

The companion `DEMO_CATALOG_IMAGE_MANIFEST.md` lists every item ID and local image provenance. The new WebP assets are original AI-generated demo menu visuals; they are not evidence of actual RIT food, stock, price or merchant permission. The commerce backend currently knows only the original 12 stable item IDs. A future integration must explicitly reconcile the 60 Android-only IDs and this presentation taxonomy with server-authoritative catalog records, photo hosting and real seller approval before production use. No backend files or API contracts were changed here.
